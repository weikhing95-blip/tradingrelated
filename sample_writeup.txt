Why I'm backing up the truck on Northgate Logistics (NGL)

I've followed Northgate for three years and written about it twice before, but
this is the first time I'm calling it my single largest position. The setup is
the best risk/reward I've seen in the small-cap logistics space since 2021.

The market still prices NGL like a sleepy regional trucker. It isn't one
anymore. Over the last 18 months management quietly built out a cross-dock
network in the Southeast and signed two anchor 3PL contracts that don't fully
hit the P&L until the back half of next year. Consensus numbers haven't caught
up — the sell-side is modeling 4% revenue growth when the contracted backlog
alone implies low-teens.

On valuation: NGL trades at roughly 7x forward EV/EBITDA versus the peer group
at 12-14x, despite a cleaner balance sheet (net debt/EBITDA under 1.5x) and a
higher incremental margin profile. If they simply execute on the backlog and
re-rate to peers, that's a double over two years without heroic assumptions.

Catalysts I'm watching: the Q3 print in November, where I expect the first
visible margin inflection; a likely contract renewal with their largest
customer in Q1; and continued debt paydown that should get them to investment-
grade-equivalent leverage by year-end.

Risks are real and I won't pretend otherwise. Freight is cyclical and a
sharper-than-expected industrial slowdown would hit volumes. The two anchor
contracts are concentrated — losing either would break the thesis. And
management has a history of over-promising on integration timelines.

But at this price, with this backlog, I think you're being paid to wait. I'm
sized accordingly.
